Heritage & Passion

The Soul of Veloura

An extraordinary gastronomic journey begins long before the first course is served.

Our Story

Veloura was founded with a singular conviction: that dining should be an awe-inspiring experience, not a simple meal. Located in Ramtali, Gujrat, Veloura has grown into a destination for diners who appreciate culinary precision, organic ingredients, and quiet luxury.

Our Culinary Philosophy

We believe culinary restraint is the highest form of respect. Every dish on our signature menu is crafted to let the finest ingredient speak — from Japanese A5 Wagyu and imported truffles to Persian saffron and organic produce.

Our Executive Chef

Trained across classical European kitchens and inspired by regional heritage, our master chef leads a dedicated brigade devoted to precision without pretension — pure technique in service of sublime flavor.

Artisanal Ingredients

We collaborate directly with small artisanal growers, trusted fishmongers, and specialty spice purveyors. Our charcoal grills use authentic binchotan charcoals to impart an unmistakable clean, smoky crust to our steaks and seafood.

Join Us For An Unforgettable Evening

Whether celebrating a milestone or enjoying an intimate dinner, Veloura welcomes you with open warmth.
