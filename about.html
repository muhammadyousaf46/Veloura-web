About Veloura

Crafted With Passion

An extraordinary dining experience begins long before the first course.

Our Story

Veloura began with a simple conviction: that a meal should be an experience, not a transaction. What started as a single dining room has grown into a destination for those who value craft, patience, and quiet luxury — without ever losing sight of the warmth that brought us here.

Our Philosophy

We believe restraint is a form of respect — for the ingredient, for the guest, for the craft itself. Every dish at Veloura is built to let its finest component speak, refined until nothing unnecessary remains.

Our Chef

Trained across classical European kitchens and shaped by years of regional exploration, our head chef leads a team devoted to precision without pretension — technique in service of flavor, always.

Our Ingredients

We work directly with growers, fishmongers, and small producers who share our standards. Seasonality dictates the menu, not the other way around — so what you taste today may not appear again until next year.

Our Restaurant

Every surface, from the dining room to the bar, was chosen for how it makes a guest feel — warm light, considered acoustics, and space to actually talk to the people you came with.

Why Veloura

Because dining out should feel like an occasion, even on an ordinary evening. That's the standard we hold ourselves to — one plate, one guest, one story at a time.
