Privacy Policy

Veloura ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information We Collect

  • Account details you provide when signing up (name, email, phone, address)
  • Order and reservation details you submit
  • Contact form messages and newsletter subscriptions

How We Use It

  • To process and fulfill your orders and reservations
  • To respond to inquiries submitted via the contact form
  • To send newsletter updates, only if you've subscribed

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely with Supabase, protected by Row Level Security policies that restrict access to your own records — with the exception of authorized administrative staff who manage orders and reservations.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us via the contact page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the email listed on our Contact page.
